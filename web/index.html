Drawbot

Terminal block labels, drawn by your Ender 3

Four quick steps and you're drawing. You'll need: a pen fixed where the nozzle was, your marker card taped flat on the bed, the printer on and plugged in over USB, and Chrome or Edge. First time? The printer needs the Drawbot firmware (step 2 checks and helps).

One label per line. L1|24V = two lines, PE@8 = a wider cell. Click a cell in the card picture to edit it.

Text direction

Switch the printer on, plug it in over USB, then connect. Chrome or Edge only.

Not connectedConnect to check which firmware your printer runs.

One zip with the patched Marlin for every supported Creality board (4.2.2, 4.2.7, 1.1.x), built from the latest Marlin, with flashing instructions and a pen-test G-code. Open the folder that matches the version printed on your board; 4.2.x boards flash from an SD card.

Marlin configs only (to build it yourself)
