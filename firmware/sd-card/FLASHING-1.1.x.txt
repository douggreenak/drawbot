DRAWBOT FIRMWARE - Creality 1.1.x board (8-bit)
================================================
The 1.1.x boards do NOT flash from an SD card. Flash the .hex over USB with avrdude, XLoader or
PlatformIO (board ATmega1284P). The stock Creality bootloader must still be on the board.

Files here:
  drawbot-....hex   the firmware (Marlin, patched for pen plotting)

NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE by the build system.
