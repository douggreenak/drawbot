DRAWBOT FIRMWARE - all supported boards in one download
=======================================================
Make a pen plotter out of an Ender 3. Pick ONE folder:

  Board-4.2.2/      Creality 4.2.2 board  (32-bit, flashes from an SD card)
  Board-4.2.7/      Creality 4.2.7 board  (32-bit, flashes from an SD card)
  Board-1.1.x-8bit/ Creality 1.1.x board  (8-bit, flashed over USB, NOT from SD)

How do I know my board?
  Switch the printer off, open the bottom cover and read the label printed on the main board:
  "V4.2.2", "V4.2.7", or "1.1.4 / 1.1.5" (8-bit). The 4.2.2 and 4.2.7 firmware are NOT interchangeable
  (their motor pins differ), so check the label before flashing.
  The 4.2.x builds run on both the STM32F103RET6 and RCT6 chips. Boards with a GD32 chip are not supported.

Then open the folder and follow READ-ME.txt inside it.

Also here: pen-test-z-lift.gcode / pen-test-servo.gcode - copy one to the SD card and print it to draw a
20 mm test square. NOT TESTED ON HARDWARE by the build system.
